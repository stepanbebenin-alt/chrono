🔥1. Fever & Pain Relievers (Antipyretics)

💧2. Hydration & Electrolytes (Oral Rehydration)

Small frequent sips protect kidneys and prevent dehydration during fever.

Daily fluid target: 1520 ml / day

Oral rehydration schedule: 1–2 teaspoons (5–10 ml) every 5–10 minutes to avoid triggering vomiting.

WHO ORS Solutions: Pedialyte, Dioralyte, Hydralyte, or standard ORS packets.

🛡️3. Antihistamines & GI Support

Dimetindene drops / Fenistil (from 1 month)

Daily dose: 2 drops per kg of body weight, divided into 3 doses per day.

For 19 kg: 13 drops 3 times daily.

Cetirizine drops / Zyrtec (oral drops)

6–12 mo: 5 drops once daily (2.5 mg). 1–2 yr: 5 drops 1–2x/day. 2–6 yr: 10 drops once daily. 6+ yr: 20 drops.

Diosmectite / Polysorb (GI adsorbents)

For vomiting, diarrhea, or mild allergies (consult doctor): <10 kg — 1 tsp/day; 11–20 kg — 1 heaped tsp per dose; 21–30 kg — 1 dessert spoon.

Where do these calculations come from? (Clinical Sources)

All dosages and formulas follow internationally recognized pediatric clinical guidelines:

  • WHO (World Health Organization): Pocket Book of Hospital Care for Children.
  • British National Formulary: BNF for Children (BNFC) / NICE Guidelines (Fever in under 5s).
  • American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP): Fever and Antipyretic Use in Children.
  • Pediatric Consensus Guidelines: Weight-based fever & ARI management protocols (2024).
⚠️ Golden Rule of Pediatrics: Antipyretic doses are calculated STRICTLY BY CHILD WEIGHT (10 mg/kg Ibuprofen, 15 mg/kg Acetaminophen/Paracetamol), not solely by age on packaging! Always confirm dosing with your pediatrician.
